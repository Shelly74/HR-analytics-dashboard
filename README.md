# HR-analytics-dashboard
An HR analytics dashboard is a business intelligence tool that visualizes workforce data—such as hiring, turnover, salary, and engagement—to help HR teams make data-driven decisions. These interactive dashboards allow users to track key performance indicators (KPIs) and drill down into details to improve employee performance and recruitment.
Key Components and MetricsEmployee Demographics: Visualization of age, gender, diversity, and experience levels across the company.Recruitment Analytics: Tracking time-to-hire, cost-per-hire, and applicant sourcing effectiveness.Turnover & Retention: Monitoring resignation rates, identifying top performers at risk, and understanding the "who" and "why" behind attrition.Compensation & Payroll: Analyzing salary distribution, pay equity, and gender pay gaps.Performance Management: Tracking employee ratings and training progress.
Key BenefitsStrategic Decision-Making: Helps align workforce strategies with business goals, such as identifying if hiring is keeping pace with turnover.Improved Efficiency: Replaces manual, fragmented reports with real-time, centralized data.Proactive Management: Allows managers to identify trends like high absenteeism or turnover risks early, as seen on Tableau's Employee Turnover dashboard.Common ToolsPower BI: Popular for interactive reports and data modeling.Tableau: Commonly used for deep exploration of workforce trends.Excel: Used for basic reporting and smaller datasets.
Drill-Down Capability: Ensure the dashboard allows users to go from high-level summaries down to individual employee profiles.Focus on Metrics: Structure the dashboard around specific goals, such as improving diversity or reducing onboarding time.
